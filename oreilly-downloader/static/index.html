Download books from your subscription

Your credentials are sent directly to O'Reilly and never stored.

How to get your session token:
  1. Open learning.oreilly.com in your browser
  2. Log in using your institution's SSO
  3. Press F12 to open DevTools
  4. Go to Application → Cookies → https://learning.oreilly.com
  5. Find the cookie named groot_sessionid (or sessionid) and copy its Value

The token is valid until your browser session expires. You can get a fresh one by repeating the steps above.
